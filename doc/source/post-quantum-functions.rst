Post-quantum Functions
======================

Post-quantum cryptography introduces future-proof security methods that will be reliable and trustworthy even once quantum computers become available. These new methods use different kinds of mathematical problems that quantum computers would struggle to break. Lattice-based mathematical problems and hash-based algorithms are common approaches to staying ahead of the evolution of quantum computers.
